About

I am a PhD candidate in Applied Mathematics at MISTEA, INRAE, in Montpellier, France working on robust digital twins to improve decision-making during crises. I'm driven by questions of how to make control systems reliable and usable when we have incomplete information about system states, and how to solve optimization problems that balance competing objectives like minimizing damage while constraining control effort
